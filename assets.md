# Asset briefs — Bony Amin homepage

20 illustrated assets, all from one visual world so they can be generated as a single
coherent set. Every slot is already wired into the wireframe at the file path below —
drop the real file in and the layout does not change.

---

## Shared style rules (apply to every asset)

**Medium and look.** Soft watercolour washes laid over fine, slightly imperfect ink
linework. Hand-painted, not vector. Visible cold-press paper grain throughout, pigment
pooling and darkening naturally at the edge of each wash, occasional wash bleeding just
past a line. Lines are thin, confident and a little wobbly — drawn by hand with a dip
pen, never ruler-straight.

**Palette.** Near-monochrome and warm. Warm paper-white ground (#FAF8F4-ish), washes in
warm grey and graphite, linework in a soft near-black ink. One single muted accent —
a dusty terracotta — used sparingly on no more than one small object per image. No
saturated colour, no gradients, no neon, no digital glow.

**Light.** Soft natural daylight from a consistent direction: window light entering from
the upper left in every daytime scene. Shadows are soft, warm-grey, short. Night scenes
are lit by two warm desk lamps only.

**Composition.** Generous negative space. No hard frame, border or outline around the
image — washes fade softly into the paper at the edges. No text, no lettering, no logos,
no UI copy of any kind inside the artwork. No drop shadows, no 3D rendering, no
photographic realism.

**The one place: the two-desk workshop.** Every asset is a scene from, or an object on,
a single imagined workspace — a quiet upstairs workshop with exactly two drafting desks
pushed together, tall windows onto city rooftops, a plan chest, a shelf of labelled
boxes, a wall of pinned drawings, one plant on the windowsill. Nothing appears that
could not plausibly live in that room. No abstract shapes, no floating dashboards, no
generic tech iconography.

**People.** At most two simplified human figures, always seen from behind or in
silhouette, loosely painted, no facial features and no detailed hands. The room is the
subject; the figures are scale and warmth only.

**Delivery.** `.webp`, transparent or warm-paper background as noted, long edge 2400px
for wide scenes, 1600px for mid-size, 600px for spots, 256px for icons.

### Hero

| | |
|---|---|
| **ID** | `HERO-SCENE` |
| **Path** | `/public/assets/hero-scene.webp` |
| **Ratio** | 16:6 |
| **Type** | illustration |

**Brief.** A wide, calm interior of the two-desk workshop in early-morning light. Two
drafting desks pushed together end to end, centre-right of frame. The left desk is the
design desk: an open sketchbook with loose layout thumbnails, a pencil stub, a brass
calliper, a scatter of paper offcuts. The right desk is the build desk: a slim monitor
turned three-quarters to us showing an abstract half-finished page layout as soft grey
blocks (no readable text), a low keyboard, cables tidied along the desk edge. Behind
them, tall multi-pane windows fill the upper left of the frame with soft morning light
and a quiet wash of city rooftops and chimneys beyond. A single plant on the windowsill.
Two simplified figures stand at the far desk seen from behind, mid-conversation, one
pointing at the screen. Wide-open warm-white space to the left third of the frame so
the composition breathes. One dusty terracotta accent: the mug on the left desk.
