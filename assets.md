# Asset briefs — Bony Amin homepage

20 illustrated assets, all from one visual world so they can be generated as a single
coherent set. Every slot is already wired into the wireframe at the file path below —
drop the real file in and the layout does not change.

---

## Shared style rules (apply to every asset)

**Medium and look.** Soft watercolour washes laid over fine, slightly imperfect ink
linework. Hand-painted, not vector. Visible cold-press paper grain throughout, pigment
pooling and darkening naturally at the edge of each wash, occasional wash bleeding just
past a line. Lines are thin, confident and a little wobbly — drawn by hand with a dip
pen, never ruler-straight.

**Palette.** Near-monochrome and warm. Warm paper-white ground (around #FAF8F4), washes
in warm grey and graphite, linework in a soft near-black ink. One single muted accent —
a dusty terracotta — used sparingly on no more than one small object per image. No
saturated colour, no gradients, no neon, no digital glow.

**Light.** Soft natural daylight from one consistent direction: window light entering
from the upper left in every daytime scene. Shadows are soft, warm-grey and short. The
one night scene is lit by a single warm desk lamp.

**Composition.** Generous negative space. No hard frame, border or outline around the
image — washes fade softly into the paper at the edges. No text, no lettering, no logos
and no readable UI copy anywhere in the artwork. No drop shadows, no 3D rendering, no
photographic realism.

**The one place: the workbench.** Every asset is a scene from, or an object on, a single
imagined workspace — one long workbench in a quiet upstairs room where the drawing tools
and the building tools share the same surface. Sketchbook and calliper at one end, screen
and keyboard at the other, a shelf of labelled boxes above, a plan chest beneath, tall
multi-pane windows onto city rooftops, one plant on the windowsill. Nothing appears that
could not plausibly live on or around that bench. No abstract shapes, no floating
dashboards, no generic tech iconography.

**People.** At most one simplified human figure, always seen from behind or in
silhouette, loosely painted, with no facial features and no detailed hands. The work on
the bench is the subject; the figure is scale and warmth only.

**Delivery.** `.webp`, warm-paper or transparent background as noted. Long edge 2400px
for wide scenes, 1600px for mid-size, 600px for spots, 256px for icons.

## Slot briefs

### Hero

| | |
|---|---|
| **ID** | `HERO-SCENE` |
| **Path** | `/public/assets/hero-scene.webp` |
| **Ratio** | 16:6 |
| **Type** | illustration |

**Brief.** A wide, calm interior of the room in early-morning light, centred on one long
workbench running across the right two-thirds of frame. At its left end, the drawing
work: an open sketchbook with loose layout thumbnails, a pencil stub, a brass calliper, a
scatter of paper offcuts. At its right end, the building work: a slim monitor turned
three-quarters to us showing an abstract half-finished page layout as soft grey blocks
(no readable text), a low keyboard, cables tidied along the bench edge. The two ends are
unmistakably the same surface, with nothing dividing them — that continuity is the whole
point of the image. Behind the bench, tall multi-pane windows fill the upper left with
soft morning light and a quiet wash of rooftops and chimneys beyond. One plant on the
windowsill. No figure. Wide-open warm-white space in the left third so the composition
breathes and the headline has room. One dusty terracotta accent: the mug at the drawing
end.
