Services

Three ways to work with us.

Product UX review

Understand what is getting in the way before committing to a redesign. We review one agreed workflow, identify usability issues, and prioritize improvements based on user needs and business context.

You receive: a focused review, prioritized findings, and a recommended scope for the next step.

Design and build sprint

Take a defined product improvement from idea to implementation. We align on the problem, design the experience, and build the agreed solution within a clear scope.

You receive: agreed design deliverables and implementation, with milestones, testing, and handover defined before the project starts.

Ongoing product improvement

Keep improving your product without restarting the vendor search for every feature. We support a planned stream of UX improvements, feature design, and development under a monthly agreement.

You receive: agreed monthly capacity, a prioritized backlog, regular communication, and visibility into what is being delivered.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do you only design, or can you build the solution too?

We bring design and development together where the project scope and our technical capabilities are a fit. We agree on exactly what will be designed, built, tested, and handed over before work begins.

Can you work with our in-house developers?

Yes. We can collaborate with your team on design, implementation, specifications, or a clearly defined part of the product roadmap.

Can you redesign our entire product?

Potentially, but we recommend starting with the workflows and user needs that matter most. We'll assess the scope before recommending a phased approach.

How do you price projects?

Pricing depends on the problem, scope, complexity, timeline, and delivery responsibilities. We provide a defined proposal before work begins.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes, when there is a good fit. Monthly engagements can cover an agreed amount of product design and development, with priorities and capacity defined in advance.

What do you need from us to get started?

A product walkthrough, context about your users and goals, access to the relevant stakeholders, and agreement on the problem to solve.

What part of your product needs to work better?

Tell us about the workflow, feature, or experience you want to improve. We'll discuss the problem and whether we're the right team to help.

Tell us what you want to improve.

Share a little about your product and the challenge. We'll review the details and get back to you about the next step.
