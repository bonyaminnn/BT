FAQ

Common questions.

Do you only design, or can you build the solution too?

We bring design and development together where the project scope and our technical capabilities are a fit. We agree on exactly what will be designed, built, tested, and handed over before work begins.

Can you work with our in-house developers?

Yes. We can collaborate with your team on design, implementation, specifications, or a clearly defined part of the product roadmap.

Can you redesign our entire product?

Potentially, but we recommend starting with the workflows and user needs that matter most. We'll assess the scope before recommending a phased approach.

How do you price projects?

Pricing depends on the problem, scope, complexity, timeline, and delivery responsibilities. We provide a defined proposal before work begins.

Do you offer ongoing support?

Yes, when there is a good fit. Monthly engagements can cover an agreed amount of product design and development, with priorities and capacity defined in advance.

What do you need from us to get started?

A product walkthrough, context about your users and goals, access to the relevant stakeholders, and agreement on the problem to solve.
