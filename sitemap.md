# Gridmarc — Phase 2 sitemap

## Pages

| Path | Page | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `/` | Home | Gives the value proposition in one screen, then shows services, a glimpse of work and how we work, and ends at contact. |
| `/services` | Services overview | Lays out everything we do in one place and sends each visitor to the right service. |
| `/services/web-design` | Web design | Covers sites and landing pages designed to convert, either a redesign of an existing site or a new one. |
| `/services/web-development` | Web development | Covers fast, maintainable builds and work inside an existing codebase or CMS. |
| `/services/app-design-development` | App design and development | Covers web and mobile apps from first screens to shipped product. |
| `/services/support` | Product support and care | Covers ongoing improvement, fixes and new features after launch, as a monthly plan. |
| `/work` | Work | Shows a short, curated set of projects, each with the problem, what we did and the result. |
| `/work/[project]` | Case study (template) | Tells one project in depth: the starting point, decisions, screens and outcome. |
| `/process` | How we work | Lays out our four steps, what happens in week one and how we communicate, to remove doubts about working with two people. |
| `/about` | About | Introduces the two people behind Gridmarc, how we split the work and why we stay small on purpose. |
| `/pricing` | Plans | Explains the three ways to work with us (Sprint, Build, Care) and what each includes. |
| `/contact` | Contact | Short brief form plus a booking link, with a clear promise of when we will reply. |
| `/faq` | FAQ | Answers the doubts about size, timelines, ownership, hand-over and support. |
| `/privacy` | Privacy policy | Required legal page. |
| `/terms` | Terms | Required legal page. |
| `/404` | Not found | Friendly dead end that links back home and to contact, using a small illustration from the world. |

## Homepage sections, in order

The rhythm follows the reference: a wide illustrated scene, then quiet type, alternating image and text, a lineup, a small breather, plans, questions, a close, and a wide illustrated scene at the bottom.

| # | Section | What it does | Slots |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Navigation** | Logo, Services, Work, Process, About, Pricing, plus the "Start a project" button. | `LOGO-MARK` |
| 2 | **Hero** | Headline, one supporting line, primary button (plus a quiet secondary link) and a wide illustrated scene. | `HERO-SCENE` |
| 3 | **Statement** | A short positioning paragraph in large type. No image. | none |
| 4 | **Three feature blocks** | The three key messages, alternating image left and right. | `FEATURE-MAKERS`, `FEATURE-SPEED`, `FEATURE-WHEREVER` |
| 5 | **Services lineup** | Four services as cards, each with a spot icon. | `ICON-WEB-DESIGN`, `ICON-WEB-DEV`, `ICON-APP`, `ICON-SUPPORT` |
| 6 | **Selected work** | A glimpse of three projects with a link to `/work`. | `WORK-01`, `WORK-02`, `WORK-03` (product shots) |
| 7 | **How we work** | Four steps, each with a small icon. | `STEP-SURVEY`, `STEP-PLAN`, `STEP-BUILD`, `STEP-TEND` |
| 8 | **Breather** | A small centred spot illustration with one line of text. | `BREATHER-STAKE` |
| 9 | **Plans** | Sprint, Build and Care, each with a small spot illustration. | `PLAN-SPRINT`, `PLAN-BUILD`, `PLAN-CARE` |
| 10 | **The two of us** | Two illustrated portraits with roles. Answers "who will I work with?" | `TEAM-ONE`, `TEAM-TWO` |
| 11 | **FAQ** | Six questions, handled as objections. | none |
| 12 | **Closing call to action** | Final ask with a small spot illustration. | `CTA-LANTERN` |
| 13 | **Footer** | Wide illustrated scene (the valley at dusk), links and contact. | `FOOTER-SCENE` |

**Totals:** 21 illustrated assets (logo mark, hero scene, 3 feature illustrations, 4 service icons, 4 step icons, breather, 3 plan spots, 2 portraits, call-to-action spot, footer scene) and 3 product shots for real project screenshots. That is 24 slots in all.
